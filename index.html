Jugo Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

In short: Jugo has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking SDKs of its own. Your walks, photos and your dog's profile stay on your iPhone — Jugo never uploads them to us. We only see what you choose to send us, such as an email or beta feedback.

What Jugo stores, and where

Everything you create in Jugo is saved only on your device:

  • Your walks: the route recorded while a walk is running, its time, distance, and the moments and photos you add.
  • Your dog's profile: name, breed, birthday and photo.
  • Your home location and the places you mark on the map.
  • Your settings and walk-time reminders.

Jugo has no server and no account system, so there is nowhere else for this data to go. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or a computer, iOS may include Jugo's data in that backup, under your own Apple settings.

Apple services Jugo uses

Some features rely on Apple services, which receive limited information to work. This goes to Apple, not to us, and is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy:

  • Maps (Apple Maps): showing the map loads map data for the area on screen.
  • Search and nearby suggestions (Apple Maps): when you search for a place or an address, your typed search and an approximate area are sent to Apple Maps. To suggest nearby places (for example while you set up Jugo or browse Places), Jugo also sends general category searches such as "park" or "coffee" with an approximate area around your home or map.
  • Weather (Apple Weather / WeatherKit): when a walk starts and ends, Jugo asks for the weather at that spot, which sends that location to Apple.
  • Marking your home: when you confirm your home, Jugo looks up the street address under your pin once, using Apple's geocoder.

Permissions

  • Location: used to draw your route during a walk, notice when you reach your places, and add the weather. Jugo asks for "While Using the App"; a walk keeps recording while your screen is locked, and iOS shows the location indicator while it does.
  • Camera: to take photos during a walk.
  • Photos: to save the photos you take to your library, if you allow it. Photos you choose from your library are picked through Apple's photo picker; Jugo does not get access to your whole library.
  • Notifications: only for reminders you turn on, such as walk times. They are scheduled and delivered on your iPhone.

You can change any of these in iOS Settings at any time.

Beta testing through TestFlight

If you test Jugo through Apple's TestFlight, Apple provides us with beta testing information as described in Apple's TestFlight documentation: crash reports, the feedback and screenshots you choose to send, basic device information, and usage figures such as installs and sessions. If you were invited by email, we can also see the name and email address used for the invitation; testers who join through a public link are generally anonymous to us. We use this only to fix problems and improve Jugo.

What we receive from you

The only personal information we receive is what you send us: support emails, TestFlight feedback, and anything you attach to them (for example a screenshot or an exported walk file). We use it only to respond to you and improve Jugo, keep it no longer than needed for that, and delete it when you ask. To request deletion, email us from the address you wrote from.

Your control

  • Delete individual walks in the app.
  • Export your walks as a file from Settings, and share it only if you choose to.
  • Deleting Jugo from your iPhone deletes the data Jugo stores on that device. It does not remove photos you saved to your Photos library, files you exported, device backups, or anything you have already sent us.

Children

Jugo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly receive personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
